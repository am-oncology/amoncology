Radiotherapy

Radiotherapy uses precisely targeted radiation to treat cancer and certain benign conditions. Dr Mahmood plans and delivers external beam radiotherapy, with particular expertise in stereotactic techniques (SRS and SABR) for intracranial, skull base and spinal disease, and in contact X-ray brachytherapy (Papillon CXB) for selected early rectal cancers.

Systemic Anti-Cancer Therapy

Systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) covers the drug treatments used in cancer care, including chemotherapy, immunotherapy and targeted agents. Dr Mahmood prescribes and supervises systemic treatment for gastrointestinal and neurological cancers, alongside supportive care to manage symptoms and treatment side effects.

Biography & Expertise

Professional profile

Dr Asad Mahmood is a Consultant Clinical Oncologist at Barts Health NHS Trust, specialising in the management of gastrointestinal and neurological cancers. His practice encompasses both systemic anti-cancer therapies and advanced radiotherapy techniques, including stereotactic radiosurgery and radiotherapy (SRS/SABR) and contact brachytherapy (Papillon CXB).

He graduated from Barts and The London with distinction, and completed medical training in London and the East of England, followed by specialty training in oncology in London.

He was awarded a Doctoral Clinical Fellowship at the Francis Crick Institute to research cancer metabolism, and received an MSc in oncology from the Institute of Cancer Research. He is a Clinical Educator in oncology and holds a postgraduate qualification in teaching and learning.

Urgent medical advice

This website is not monitored for emergencies and must not be used to report urgent symptoms. If you are undergoing cancer treatment and feel unwell — for example with a fever, chest pain or uncontrolled symptoms — contact the 24-hour acute oncology service number given to you by your treating team.

In a life-threatening emergency, dial 999 or attend your nearest A&E department immediately.
